Online Dua Counter

Tasbii's Dua Counter lets you select a dua and track repeated recitations with a custom target. It works directly in your browser and does not require an account.

Open the Dua Counter →

How to use it

Select the dua you want to recite, choose your target, and tap the counter as you complete each repetition.

Useful while travelling

A browser-based counter is easy to access while travelling or commuting because there is nothing extra to install.
