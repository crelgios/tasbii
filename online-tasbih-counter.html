Online Tasbih Counter

Tasbii is a free digital Tasbih counter that works directly in your browser. Use it for dhikr, remembrance and reciting the 99 Names of Allah without creating an account.

How to use the digital Tasbih

Choose a Name of Allah, tap the counter as you recite, and your progress is saved automatically on your device. The interface is designed for phones and desktops.

What is a Tasbih counter?

A Tasbih counter helps you keep track of repeated dhikr and recitations. A digital counter can be useful when travelling or when prayer beads are not available.
